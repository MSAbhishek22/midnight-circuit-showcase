How it works

Show up. Build. Screen it.

No slides about your idea — the demo is the pitch. Teams of two to four, or fly solo and find your crew at the door.

  1. Register and get your paper ticket by email
  2. Arrive at 18:00 — teams lock at 20:00 sharp
  3. Build for 22 hours, with mentors on the floor all night
  4. Demo live at 18:00 next day — three minutes, no exceptions
